# AI-assisted contributions

AI tools are welcome in Catena. The same review requirements apply to every contribution.

1. **Before opening a pull request**, personally review the entire diff, understand the changes, and run the relevant checks yourself. You are responsible for the quality, correctness, and licensing of what you submit, for explaining the changes, and for addressing review feedback.
2. **Before merging**, a maintainer or an authorised contributor other than the author must personally review and approve the changes.

Automated checks and AI reviews can support both reviews. Both reviews must be performed by people.
